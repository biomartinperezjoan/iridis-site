03

Science validation & evidence audit

AI made building health products easy. It did not make the science behind them reliable. Before your app, landing page or pitch deck reaches users, investors or clinicians, we check whether the claims, the references and the evidence actually support what the product says.

  • Scientific claim auditWe review your web, app copy or deck and map every claim against the available evidence. Deliverable: claim matrix with risk levels and reformulations.
  • Evidence mappingThe science behind your product, module by module: what's supported, what's extrapolated, what would need a study. Deliverable: evidence dossier.
  • Scientific positioning & messagingRewriting claims and narrative so they sound credible because they are credible — no pseudoscientific shortcuts.
  • Pre-launch scientific reviewClaim audit + evidence mapping + user-flow review, combined, before you go to market.
Product claimEvidenceRisk
"Improves sleep quality" Indirect studies; acceptable if qualified Defensible
"Reduces anxiety" Weak or extrapolated evidence Needs rewording
