Why Iridis

The colour is in the structure, not the surface.

The hummingbird’s green is not pigment — it is structure. A scientific text works the same way: the same data can dazzle or go unnoticed depending on how it is built.

Structural clarityThe argument and the hierarchy of evidence are what a reviewer, an investor or a client reads first.
The gapBetween what a study says and what a headline reports. We close it without losing rigour.
Precision in flightThe hummingbird hovers perfectly still — controlled force. Rigour and clarity enable each other.

Services

Three lines of work.
One standard.

Every deliverable starts from the same principle: the structure does the work. We don't sell words — we build and verify arguments that hold up in front of a reviewer, an investor or a feed algorithm.

01

Publication support

From data to accepted manuscript. We work with your results and your team so the paper says exactly what the evidence allows — and reads the way it deserves.

Medical writing Peer review
  • ManuscriptsFull papers from data and protocol: IMRaD structure, results narrative, honest discussion. Substantive editing of existing drafts too.
  • ReviewsNarrative and systematic reviews: search strategy, evidence synthesis, and a structure that earns the citation.
  • Responses to reviewersPoint-by-point letters that concede what should be conceded — and defend what shouldn't.
  • Journal submission packagesCover letter, formatting to journal requirements, graphical abstract and highlights. Ready to submit.
02

Scientific content & medical communications

Your team knows the science. We turn it into material that builds authority over time: for clients, for investors, for the field. Judgement, not just presence.

  • White papersResearch, argument structure and writing. The long document is where credibility is won — and it works for years.
  • Technical blogs and newslettersRecurring editorial content a scientist can read without raising an eyebrow.
  • LinkedIn ghostwritingPosts and articles for founders, researchers and company pages — written in your voice, from interviews and your own material.
  • Scientific decksPitch and conference presentations: the science first, the design in service of the argument.
03

Science validation & evidence audit

AI made building health products easy. It didn't make the science reliable. We check whether claims, references and evidence actually support what the product says — before it reaches users, investors or clinicians.

Note: This is scientific review of claims and evidence — not regulatory certification or legal assessment. We'll flag when regulatory counsel is needed.

  • Claim auditEvery claim mapped against evidence. Deliverable: claim matrix with risk levels and reformulations.
  • Evidence mappingThe science behind your product, module by module. Deliverable: evidence dossier.
  • Pre-launch reviewClaim audit + evidence mapping + user-flow review, combined, before you go to market.
Product claimEvidenceRisk
"Improves sleep quality" Indirect studies; acceptable if qualified Defensible
"Reduces anxiety" Weak or extrapolated Needs rewording
"Detects early signs of depression" Requires clinical validation Medical device territory
