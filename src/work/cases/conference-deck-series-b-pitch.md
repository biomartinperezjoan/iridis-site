## The situation

The company was preparing for a Series B and needed a scientific deck that could accompany their investor presentation. The problem: the science was complex and the investor audience was non-specialist. Previous versions either oversimplified to the point of being unconvincing, or remained too technical to land.

## What we did

We restructured the narrative arc: starting from the clinical problem, building to the mechanism, then the evidence, then the platform's position in the landscape. Each slide was reviewed for the assumption it was making about the reader — if the assumption was too technical, the content was rebuilt from a different starting point.

Figures were reviewed for interpretability. Where the science required context, we added it. Where it was being overclaimed, we pulled back.

## The outcome

The deck was used across Series B investor meetings. The feedback from the company was that investors were engaging with the science rather than waiting for it to end.
