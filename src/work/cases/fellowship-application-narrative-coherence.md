## The situation

The candidate had strong credentials and a compelling research idea but the application read as a CV summary rather than a scientific argument. The connection between the proposed research and the fellowship's priorities was implicit rather than explicit.

## What we did

We restructured the personal statement to lead with the research question and its significance, introducing the candidate's background as evidence of capacity to deliver rather than as the focus itself.

The host institution fit section was rewritten to show a genuine intellectual alignment, not just institutional prestige. Each section was edited to use the scheme's own language where it matched the candidate's genuine profile.

## The outcome

The application was submitted with a substantially clearer narrative. The connection between candidate, host, and research programme was explicit throughout.
