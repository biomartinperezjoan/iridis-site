## The situation

The research was solid and the manuscript was written. What the team needed was everything around it: a cover letter that framed the work for the editor, a graphical abstract that communicated the finding visually, and a submission formatted precisely to the journal's author guidelines.

## What we did

We wrote the cover letter to address the journal's specific scope and readership, positioning the paper's contribution clearly within the field. The graphical abstract was designed to work both in print and digital formats. Formatting was checked and corrected against the journal's current submission requirements.

## The outcome

Accepted on the first submission attempt. No desk rejection, no formatting-related delays.
