## The situation

A reviewer raised a fundamental challenge to the study's analytical approach, suggesting the methodology was insufficient to support the conclusions. The authors had strong scientific grounds to defend their choices but struggled to articulate the rebuttal without sounding defensive.

## What we did

We mapped each reviewer comment to the specific section of the paper it referenced. For the methods critique, we drafted a response that reframed the analytical approach within the broader literature — showing not just that the method was valid, but that it was the appropriate choice given the study design.

Where minor clarifications strengthened the case, we revised the corresponding manuscript sections and flagged them in the response.

## The outcome

The editor accepted the rebuttal. No additional experiments were required. The paper was accepted at the next decision round.
