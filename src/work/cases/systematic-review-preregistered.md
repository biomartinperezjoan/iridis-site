## The situation

The research team had completed the data extraction and had a clear clinical question. What they lacked was the structured writing and methodological framing a systematic review requires — PRISMA compliance, pre-registration write-up, and a results narrative that held across heterogeneous studies.

## What we did

We drafted the full manuscript following PRISMA 2020 guidelines. The search strategy section was written to match the pre-registered protocol. The results were structured as a synthesis rather than a study-by-study list, drawing out patterns across the evidence base.

The discussion was scoped to what the data could actually support — no overclaiming, no hedging where the evidence was clear.

## The outcome

Published in a specialty journal. The paper received its first citations within months of release.
