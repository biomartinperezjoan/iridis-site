## The situation

The science was well-described in the technical sections. The problem was the impact and lay summary — written at the same level as the rest of the proposal, they were inaccessible to the non-specialist panel members who would score them.

## What we did

We rewrote both sections from scratch. The lay summary was restructured around the problem the research addresses, not the method used to address it. Jargon was replaced throughout — not simplified away, but translated into language that preserved precision while gaining accessibility.

The impact section was rewritten to be specific: named beneficiaries, named pathways, named timeframes. Vague claims about potential were replaced with concrete statements about how and for whom the work would matter.

## The outcome

Both sections cleared the internal review stage and the application advanced to full panel assessment.
