## The situation

The client had a strong dataset but the manuscript had been rejected twice — first desk-rejected, then after peer review. Reviewers consistently flagged the same structural issue: the scientific rationale for the study design was buried, and the results section read as a data dump rather than a structured argument.

## What we did

We rebuilt the manuscript from the outline out. The introduction was rewritten to foreground the research gap. The results section was restructured so each finding led logically to the next, building toward the discussion rather than listing independently. The discussion was tightened to avoid overclaiming.

We also drafted a cover letter that addressed the previous rejection directly, showing the editor what had changed.

## The outcome

The revised manuscript passed peer review with minor revisions on the first submission to the new journal. Accepted within the target quarter.
