## The situation

The application had been unsuccessful in the previous round. Panel feedback pointed to a scientific rationale that felt underdeveloped and a work plan that didn't convincingly map onto the stated objectives.

## What we did

We rebuilt the application section by section. The scientific rationale was rewritten to make the research gap explicit before introducing the proposed approach. The work plan was restructured so each work package connected clearly to a specific objective, with realistic milestones and a deliverables table reviewers could follow.

The expected impact section was also rewritten to speak directly to the funder's priorities.

## The outcome

The resubmitted application presented a substantially stronger case. Outcome pending at the time of publication.
