## The situation

The client had received detailed reviewer feedback after an unsuccessful application. The feedback was contradictory in places and it wasn't clear how to address it without undermining other parts of the proposal.

## What we did

We analysed the reviewer comments systematically — identifying which were substantive scientific concerns, which were misreadings of the original text, and which were contradictions to navigate rather than resolve. This became the resubmission strategy document.

We then rewrote the sections flagged most frequently, and drafted a summary of changes that would be submitted alongside the revised application.

## The outcome

A coherent resubmission strategy and revised narrative, delivered within the scheme's resubmission window.
