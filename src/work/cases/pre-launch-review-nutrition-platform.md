## The situation

The platform was ready to launch. The founders had scientific backgrounds but hadn't done a structured review of the product as a whole — the claims, the references, and the way the science was presented across the user journey.

## What we did

We ran a full pre-launch review: claim audit across all product copy, reference check on every cited study, and a user-flow audit reviewing how scientific information was presented at each step.

The user-flow audit was particularly useful — it identified places where technically accurate statements were presented in a context that could mislead a general audience.

## The outcome

Seven issues identified: two claim reformulations, three reference corrections, and two user-flow changes. All resolved before launch.
