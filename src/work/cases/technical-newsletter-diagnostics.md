## The situation

The company had a clinical network they wanted to stay connected to between conferences and sales cycles. A newsletter was the obvious format — but previous attempts had read like press releases and had low engagement.

## What we did

We repositioned the newsletter as a knowledge resource, not a product update. Each issue led with a clinical or methodological question the readership genuinely faced, then connected the company's work to that question rather than leading with it.

Content was planned quarterly, written monthly, and reviewed by the company's scientific team before sending.

## The outcome

Open rates above category average from issue one. The forward rate within the clinical network was particularly strong — the newsletter was being shared rather than filed.
