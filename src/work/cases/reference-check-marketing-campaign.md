## The situation

The marketing team had built a campaign around a set of health claims, each with a citation. The legal team flagged it before publication — they wanted to verify the citations were being used correctly.

## What we did

We retrieved and read every cited study. For each one: what the study actually showed, what population it was conducted in, what the study's own authors cautioned about generalisation, and whether the marketing claim matched what the evidence supported.

Four studies were being used to support claims they didn't make — in some cases the studies had actually shown the opposite in certain subgroups.

## The outcome

Four corrections made before the campaign went live. The revised copy was accurate, defensible, and — crucially — still commercially effective.
