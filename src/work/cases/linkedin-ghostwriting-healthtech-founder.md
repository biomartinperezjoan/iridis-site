## The situation

The founder had domain expertise and genuine things to say. The problem was time and consistency — sporadic posts that didn't build an audience, and a voice that shifted between technical and commercial without a clear register.

## What we did

We established a voice guide based on long-form conversations with the founder. Content was planned in monthly batches around three recurring themes: the clinical problem the company addresses, the evidence behind the product, and the founder's perspective on the sector.

Each post was written in the founder's voice, reviewed by them, and published under their name. No AI filler, no recycled frameworks.

## The outcome

Follower growth was measurable by month two. By month three, the founder was receiving inbound messages from potential partners and investors who had found them through LinkedIn content.
