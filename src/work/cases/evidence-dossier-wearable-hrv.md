## The situation

The company was entering Series A due diligence. Investors were asking about the scientific basis for the HRV-based features — and the company needed something more rigorous than a landing page and a few cited studies.

## What we did

We conducted a systematic review of the HRV literature relevant to the product's specific use cases. The dossier was structured as an evidence-to-claim map: each product feature connected to the research supporting it, with explicit notes on evidence quality, population generalisability, and the conditions under which the evidence held.

Where the evidence was preliminary, we said so — and explained what that meant for how the features should be represented.

## The outcome

The dossier was included in the Series A data room. Due diligence completed without scientific challenges to the product claims.
