## The situation

The company had strong platform science but no document that explained it accessibly to a non-specialist commercial audience. Their sales team was relying on pitch decks that couldn't carry the technical weight of the conversations they were having.

## What we did

We interviewed the scientific team and reviewed the underlying research. The white paper was structured in three layers: the problem the platform addresses, the science behind the solution, and the commercial implications — so it could be read selectively by readers with different levels of technical background.

Every claim was mapped against the evidence. Where the science was preliminary, we said so. The credibility of the document depended on it.

## The outcome

The white paper became the company's primary leave-behind in commercial conversations. Eighteen months after publication, it remains their most-requested document.
